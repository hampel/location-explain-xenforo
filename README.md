# Explain the Location field in XenForo

Adds a line of explanatory text beneath the Location field, on the registration form and on the
account details page — to tell members what format you want, or why you ask.

By [Simon Hampel](https://xenforo.com/community/members/sim.4264/).

## Requirements

XenForo 2.2.8 or later.

## Usage

**Setup** > **Options** > **Location Explain** — enter the text to show. It is plain text: HTML is
shown as typed, not rendered. Leave it empty and the add-on changes nothing.

**The explanation appears at registration only when the forum requires a location** — *Require
location* under **Setup** > **Options** > **User registration** > *Registration setup*. Without
it, XenForo does not show the Location field at registration at all, so there is nothing to
explain.
